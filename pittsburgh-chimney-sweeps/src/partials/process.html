How it works

Four steps. Zero surprises.

  1. Book in 60 seconds

    Online or by phone. Pick a 2-hour arrival window that works for you, including Saturdays.

  2. We protect your home

    Drop cloths, shoe covers and a HEPA vacuum go down before any brush touches your flue.

  3. Sweep & inspect

    Rotary or brush cleaning, then a top-to-bottom inspection with camera as needed.

  4. Photo report & plan

    See every finding with photos. If something needs fixing, you get a written price and no pressure.
