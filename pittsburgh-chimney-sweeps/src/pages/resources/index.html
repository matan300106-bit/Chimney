Quick answers

The short versions

The questions we hear most, answered in a sentence or two. There are more on our FAQ page.

How often should a chimney be inspected?

Once a year, whatever it burns. That's the NFPA 211 standard. Sweep when there's about 1/8 inch of buildup.

Do gas fireplaces need service?

Yes. They make little soot, but blocked vents, misplaced logs and worn parts can cause carbon monoxide problems.

Is a home inspection enough when buying?

Usually not for the chimney. Most don't look inside the flue. A Level 2 inspection with a camera does.

How often should a dryer vent be cleaned?

Clean the lint screen every load and the full vent at least once a year, sooner for long or roof-top runs.

Is it an emergency?If you think your chimney is on fire, or you smell gas, get everyone out of the house and call 911 from outside.
