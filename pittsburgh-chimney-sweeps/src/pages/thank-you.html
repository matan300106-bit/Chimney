Before we arrive

Getting your home ready

  • No fire for 24 hours. Let the fireplace or stove go cold for a full day before a sweep or inspection.
  • Clear a path. Make room from the door to the fireplace, and around the dryer for a vent cleaning.
  • Clear the mantel and hearth. Move photos, decor and fireplace tools out of the way.
  • Pets in another room. Doors stay open while we bring equipment in and out.
  • Note what you've noticed. Smells, stains, drafts or smoke. Tell the technician when they arrive.
Chimney fire right now?Get everyone out of the house and call 911. Then call us once the fire department has cleared the house.
