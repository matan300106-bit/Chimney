Signs you're due

Your chimney is telling you

  • It's been a year or more since your last sweep, or you don't know when it was
  • A campfire or tar smell from the fireplace, worse on humid days
  • Black, flaky or shiny buildup inside the firebox or on the damper
  • Smoke drifting into the room instead of going up the flue
  • Fires that are hard to start or keep going
  • You just moved in and have no record of the chimney's history

How much does a sweep cost?
It depends on the number of flues and what you burn, so we confirm the price with you in writing before any work starts. No surprise add-ons. If the inspection turns up a repair, the estimate for it is free.
Will it make a mess in my house?
No. We lay drop cloths from your door to the fireplace, wear shoe covers, and run a HEPA vacuum that keeps soot under negative pressure the whole time.
How long does it take?
Usually 45 to 90 minutes for a standard sweep and Level 1 inspection. Multi-flue chimneys or a camera inspection take a little longer. We'll give you an estimate when you book.
I only burn a few fires a year. Do I still need a sweep?
NFPA 211 calls for an inspection every year and cleaning as needed. Light use builds less creosote, but nests, debris and cracked flue tiles happen whether you burn or not. The inspection tells you if a sweep is actually needed.
Should I stop using the fireplace before you come?
Yes. Please don't light a fire for 24 hours before your appointment so the flue is cool enough to work in.
