Signs of a clogged vent

Your dryer is working too hard

  • Loads take two cycles, or clothes come out damp
  • The dryer, or the clothes, feel unusually hot
  • A burning or hot-lint smell in the laundry room
  • The outside vent flap barely opens when the dryer runs
  • Lint collecting around the outside vent or behind the dryer
  • It's been more than a year, or you've never had the vent cleaned
Lint is a fire riskThe U.S. Fire Administration lists failure to clean as the leading cause of home clothes dryer fires. On a gas dryer, a blocked vent can also push exhaust, including carbon monoxide, back into the house. Smell burning? Turn the dryer off. If you see smoke or flames, get everyone out and call 911.

How much does dryer vent cleaning cost?
It depends on how long the vent run is and where it exits. A short run through a first-floor wall is a different job from a long run to the roof. We confirm the price in writing before we start.
Will it make a mess in my laundry room?
No. We protect the floor around the dryer and use a vacuum system to capture the lint as it comes out of the vent.
How long does it take?
It depends on the length of the run and how hard it is to reach. Most homes fit easily in a single visit, and we'll give you a time estimate when you book.
How often should a dryer vent be cleaned?
Once a year is a good rule for most households. Large families, long vent runs or a vent that exits through the roof may need it more often. If you notice any of the warning signs, don't wait.
Can you do it at the same visit as my chimney sweep?
Yes. Choose either service on the form and mention the other in the notes, and we'll plan the visit for both.
