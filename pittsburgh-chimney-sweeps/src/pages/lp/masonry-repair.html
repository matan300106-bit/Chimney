What we fix

Repair what's failing. Keep what isn't.

Not every damaged chimney needs to come down. We show you exactly which sections are failing and price the repair that actually fits.

Tuckpointing

Grinding out eroded mortar joints and repointing them, so water stops getting into the brick.

Spalled brick

Replacing brick whose faces have popped off or crumbled from freeze-thaw damage.

Crown repair

Sealing or rebuilding the cracked concrete cap on top of the chimney that sheds water away from the brick.

Partial or full rebuilds

When a section is loose or leaning, we take it down to sound brick and rebuild it.

Signs your masonry needs work

What to look for from the yard

  • Mortar joints that look recessed, sandy or missing
  • Brick faces flaking, cracking or popping off
  • Bits of brick or mortar on the roof, in the gutter or in the firebox
  • Cracks in the crown, the concrete slab on top of the chimney
  • White, chalky staining (efflorescence) on the brick
  • A chimney that leans, or loose brick near the top

Is the estimate really free?
Yes. You get a written, itemized estimate with photos at no charge, and no pressure to decide on the spot.
How much will the repair cost?
It depends on how much of the chimney is affected, its height and how easy it is to reach. Repointing one section costs far less than a rebuild, which is why we price from what we actually find, not from a guess over the phone.
Do I need a full rebuild?
Often not. If the brick is sound and the damage is limited to mortar joints or the crown, tuckpointing and a crown repair can be enough. We'll show you the photos and explain the options.
Will you make a mess of my roof and yard?
Inside, drop cloths and a HEPA vacuum go down before we touch the firebox. Outside, your estimate covers how the work area will be set up and cleaned up, so you know what to expect before anything starts.
Can masonry work be done in winter?
Mortar needs the right temperatures to cure properly, so some repairs wait for milder days. We'll tell you when your repair can be done and what to watch for until then.
