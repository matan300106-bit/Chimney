Chimneys in Fox Chapel

More flues, more stone, more trees

Fox Chapel is a mostly residential borough on the hills above the Allegheny River, northeast of the city. It's known for large homes on big, heavily wooded lots, from older estate-style houses to custom homes built over the following decades.

Bigger houses usually mean bigger chimneys. It's common here to find a living room fireplace, a library or family room fireplace, sometimes one in a bedroom, plus furnace and boiler flues, all sharing one or two large stacks. Many are stone, or brick with stone details, and some sit on slate or tile roofs.

Then there are the trees. A heavy canopy keeps chimneys shaded and damp, which encourages moss and slows drying after rain. Leaves and branches collect on crowns and in uncapped flues, and wooded lots are home to plenty of raccoons, squirrels and birds looking for a sheltered spot.

Every flue, separately

Each flue gets its own cleaning, its own inspection and its own line in your photo report, so you know which fireplace is ready to use.

Stone that's repointed right

Stone chimneys need mortar suited to the stone and careful hand work, not a quick skim coat.

Caps that fit the chimney

Large multi-flue caps in stainless or copper, sized to cover every flue and keep animals and leaves out.

Services for Fox Chapel homes

Where to start

  • Camera inspection: the clearest picture of a multi-flue chimney, flue by flue, and the right first step before any repair.
  • Stone and brick repair: repointing, rebuilding damaged sections and fixing the crown on large stacks.
  • Multi-flue caps: stainless or copper, sized to the crown.
  • Animal removal: humane removal of raccoons, squirrels and birds, then a cap so they can't return.
  • Chimney liners: stainless relining when an older clay flue is cracked or a boiler or furnace is replaced.

Fox Chapel FAQ

Before you book

Tell us how many fireplaces and appliances you have when you book, and we'll set aside the right amount of time.

Our chimney has four flues. Do you clean and inspect each one?
Yes. Each flue is cleaned and inspected separately, including the ones serving the furnace or boiler. Your photo report labels each flue so you know exactly which fireplace or appliance a finding relates to.
Do you install copper chimney caps?
Yes. We install both stainless steel and copper caps, including large multi-flue caps that cover the whole crown. Copper darkens and develops a patina as it weathers, which suits stone and older brick.
We have a long, steep driveway and a lot of landscaping. Is that a problem?
No. Wooded, sloped lots are normal here. Let us know about access, gates or delicate plantings when you book, and we'll plan where to park and where ladders go.
Do you also serve O'Hara, Indiana Township and Aspinwall?
Often, yes. Those communities don't have their own page yet. Enter your ZIP code on our service areas page or call to confirm.
