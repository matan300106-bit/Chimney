Chimneys in Moon Township

Airport-corridor suburbs, built in waves

Moon Township lies along the Ohio River west of the city, next to Pittsburgh International Airport and home to Robert Morris University. It grew in stages: mid-century neighborhoods first, then newer subdivisions, townhome communities and apartment complexes along the I-376 and University Boulevard corridors.

So the chimneys vary from street to street. Older single-family homes tend to have brick chimneys with clay liners and a fireplace, often with the furnace venting up a second flue. Newer houses and townhomes are more likely to have a factory-built or gas fireplace and no masonry chimney at all.

Moon also has plenty of rental properties and townhome communities. For owners who don't live on site, clear, dated documentation of a chimney's condition is worth having.

Mid-century homes

Brick chimneys, clay liners, crown and mortar wear, and shared furnace flues.

Newer houses

Factory-built and direct-vent gas fireplaces that need yearly service and the right parts.

Townhomes & apartments

Long dryer vent runs, often through the roof, that clog faster than you'd think.

Buyers & sellers

Level 2 inspections

NFPA 211 recommends a Level 2 inspection when a property is sold, and a general home inspection doesn't look inside the flue. Ours includes a camera pass through every flue and a written report both agents can read.

Real estate inspections
Landlords & property managers

Between tenants

A turnover is the easiest time to sweep a fireplace, service a gas unit and clean the dryer vent. You get a dated photo report for your records, which is handy if an insurer or manager asks what was done.

Can you inspect before we close on a house in Moon Township?
Yes. We schedule Level 2 inspections around closing dates and send a written report with photos. If we find a problem, you also get a written repair estimate you can use in negotiations.
I own a rental. Can my tenant be home instead of me?
Yes. Just give us the tenant's contact details when you book. We'll confirm the arrival window with them and send the photo report to you.
Do you clean dryer vents in townhomes and apartments?
Yes, including vents that run to the roof. If the termination is on a shared roof, check whether your association needs to approve roof access, and let us know when you book.
