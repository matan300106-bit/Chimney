Why we exist

Chimney work you can check for yourself

Most homeowners never see the inside of their chimney. That makes it easy for a contractor to say "it's fine" when it isn't, or "it all has to come down" when it doesn't.

We built this company around closing that gap. You see what we see: the photos, the flue camera stills, the exact spot on the crown that's cracked. Then we explain it in plain English and put a price on paper. Some visits end with "you're good for another year." Others end with a repair plan. Either way, you get the evidence and the decision stays yours.

Our standards

How every job is done

The same standards apply on every visit, from a single sweep to a full rebuild.

Certified technicians

Every lead technician holds CSIA certification, which means passing exams on chimney codes, the NFPA 211 standard and safe venting practice, and keeping that certification current.

Licensed & insured

We're a registered Pennsylvania home improvement contractor ({{license}}) and fully insured for work on roofs, ladders and masonry. Property managers can ask us for proof of insurance.

Written, upfront pricing

You get the price in writing before any work begins. If we find something new partway through, we stop and talk to you before doing anything beyond what you approved.

Photo reports

Findings are photographed, labelled and ranked by urgency, then texted to you the same day. They're useful for your records, your insurer or the buyer of your home.

Your home, protected

Drop cloths from the door to the hearth, shoe covers, and a HEPA vacuum that keeps soot under negative pressure. Outside, we watch where ladders and debris land.

No pressure, no scare tactics

We won't tell you your house is about to burn down to close a sale. Real safety hazards get flagged clearly, with photos. Everything else gets an honest "this can wait" or "keep an eye on it."

Repair before replace

The smallest fix that actually solves it

A chimney with a few bad joints doesn't always need a rebuild. A leak doesn't always mean new flashing. We start with the least invasive repair that will hold, and we tell you when it won't.

  • Repoint the damaged section instead of rebuilding sound brick.
  • Repair or recoat a crown when the base is still solid, rebuild it when it isn't.
  • Find the actual leak source before replacing anything. Often it's a cap, crown or seal, not the whole flashing.
  • Explain the trade-off when a bigger job really is the better value over time.

Why only Pittsburgh

We stay close to home on purpose

We work Greater Pittsburgh and nowhere else. That focus makes us better at the job and easier to rely on.

  • We know these chimneys. Older brick, clay-tile flues, steep roofs and hillside lots are what we see week in, week out.
  • We know this weather. Repeated freeze-thaw cycles are hard on mortar and crowns, so we look for that damage on every visit.
  • Tighter arrival windows. Shorter drives between jobs mean we can hold a 2-hour window and text you when we're on the way.
  • Easy to come back. If a repair needs a second visit or a follow-up look after a storm, we're not driving in from another region.

What a visit looks like

From the first text to the photo report

Here's what happens on a typical sweep and inspection, so nothing about the day is a surprise.

  1. Before we arrive

    You get a text confirming your 2-hour arrival window, then an on-the-way text with your technician's name and photo.

  2. At your door

    Shoe covers on. Drop cloths down from the entry to the hearth. The HEPA vacuum is set up before any brush goes into the flue.

  3. The work

    We clean the flue, smoke chamber and firebox, then inspect the system top to bottom, using the camera where it's needed.

  4. Before we leave

    We walk you through what we found. The photo report follows the same day, with a free written estimate if anything needs repair.

Credentials

The paperwork behind the promise

Anyone can buy a brush. Here's what stands behind ours.

CSIA certification

The Chimney Safety Institute of America certifies sweeps through exams on codes, standards and safe practice, with recertification required to keep the credential. Every lead technician on our crews holds it.

PA home improvement registration

Pennsylvania requires home improvement contractors to register with the Attorney General's office. Our registration number is {{license}}.

Insured for the job

Chimney work happens on roofs, on ladders and inside your home. We carry insurance for that work, and we're happy to send proof to you, your HOA or your property manager.

We inspect to NFPA 211, the national standard for chimneys, fireplaces, vents and solid fuel-burning appliances.
