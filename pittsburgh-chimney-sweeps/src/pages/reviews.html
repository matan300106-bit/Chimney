What we hold ourselves to

What we want every review to say

We don't write our own reviews. What we can do is decide what we'd want you to be able to say about us, and hold every crew to it.

On time, with a heads-up

A 2-hour arrival window confirmed by text, and an on-the-way text with your technician's name and photo.

No soot left behind

Drop cloths, shoe covers and a HEPA vacuum on every job. Your living room should look the way we found it.

Photos of what we found

A photo report texted the same day, so you can see the inside of your chimney for yourself.

A price before the work

Every job is priced in writing before it starts. Repair estimates are free, itemized and come with photos.

Honest repair advice

We recommend the smallest repair that will actually hold, and we say so when something can wait.

A real person on the phone

Call or text and you reach our office, not a call center. Booking requests get a reply within one business hour.
