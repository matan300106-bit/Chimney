Is my appointment booked once I send the form?
Not quite. The form is a request. We text you within one business hour to confirm the service, the price and your 2-hour arrival window. If you sent it outside business hours, we'll reply when we open.
Do I need to be home?
For anything inside the house, such as a sweep, inspection or dryer vent, someone needs to let us in. For some exterior repairs we only need access to the roof and yard. We'll confirm when we text you.
Can I book a Saturday?
Yes. Saturday appointments are available, and our current hours are listed at the top of this page.
Not sure which service you need?
Pick "Not sure" on the form and tell us what you're seeing. We'll recommend the right visit. You can also check the services page.
How do I prepare for the visit?
Don't use the fireplace or stove for 24 hours before a sweep or inspection, clear a path to the fireplace, and move anything off the mantel and hearth. Keep pets in another room while we work.
