The whole system

Every part of your chimney, and who looks after it

A chimney is more than a brick column. It's a venting system: the fireplace or appliance at the bottom, a liner that carries exhaust up, masonry that holds it all together, and a crown, cap and flashing that keep the weather out.

When one part fails, it usually affects another. A missing cap lets rain rust the damper. A cracked crown lets water into the brick. A new furnace can leave a water heater in a flue that's too big for it. That's why we look at the whole thing on every visit, and why it helps to have one company that can fix any part of it.

Not sure where to start? Book a sweep or an inspection. You'll get a photo report of the whole system and a written price for anything that needs work.
