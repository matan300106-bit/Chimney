The roles

Where you could fit

We're not always hiring for every position, but we're always glad to hear from good people. Tell us which role interests you.

Chimney technician

Run sweeps and inspections, operate the flue camera, explain findings to homeowners and write up clear photo reports. You'll be the face of the company in people's living rooms.

  • Chimney, HVAC, roofing or other trade experience helps
  • CSIA certification, or willingness to earn it with our training
  • Comfortable on ladders and roofs

Apprentice / helper

Work alongside a lead technician: set up drop cloths and HEPA vacuums, handle ladders and equipment, and learn sweeping and inspection from the ground up. This is the starting point for a technician career.

  • No chimney experience needed
  • Reliable, on time and ready to learn
  • Comfortable with physical work and heights

Masonry technician

Repoint and rebuild chimneys, repair and pour crowns, and install caps and flashing. Pittsburgh's older brick and freeze-thaw winters keep this work interesting.

  • Brick and mortar experience
  • Careful, clean work at height
  • Pride in repairs that blend in and last

Office & scheduling

Answer calls and texts, book appointments, build routes, and keep customers updated. You're the reason we can reply within one business hour and hold a 2-hour arrival window.

  • Friendly, clear phone manner
  • Organized and comfortable with scheduling software
  • Customer service experience helps
What we look for

We can teach the trade. Character we can't.

Our customers let us into their homes and trust what we tell them. The people we hire have to deserve that.

  • Honesty. You report what you see, including "this is fine." No scare tactics, ever.
  • Respect for people's homes. Shoe covers on, drop cloths down, nothing left behind.
  • Reliability. Our arrival windows only work if every crew shows up on time.
  • Clear communication. You can explain a cracked flue tile to a homeowner without jargon.
  • Safety first. You take ladders, roofs and carbon monoxide seriously.
What we offer

A trade with a future

Paid training toward CSIA

We train you on the job and support you through the Chimney Safety Institute of America certification, the credential every one of our lead technicians holds.

Steady, year-round work

Sweeps and inspections peak in fall and winter. Masonry, caps and crowns pick up when the weather warms. Dryer vents and leak repairs keep going all year.

Close to home

We only work in Greater Pittsburgh, so your day is spent on jobs, not long drives across the state.
